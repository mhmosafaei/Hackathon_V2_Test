DEMONSTRATION CONTRACT — NOT A PROJECT CONTRACT

1. Marine operations shall not proceed where significant wave height exceeds 1.5 m.
2. Operations require wind speed not to exceed 30 km/h.
3. Operations require wind gusts not to exceed 45 km/h.
4. A continuous workable weather window of 2 hours is required before mobilisation.
5. Weather standby shall be recorded with contemporaneous forecast evidence. Weather risk is contractor risk.
6. Any extension of time for adverse weather is subject to written notice and human contractual assessment.
